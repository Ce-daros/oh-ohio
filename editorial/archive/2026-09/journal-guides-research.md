# Guide article research

Checked 2026-09-24. These notes document the factual basis for the four original guide articles in `site/src/data/journal-guides.json`. Itinerary order and voice are editorial synthesis; none of the prose claims a personal visit. Dynamic schedules, tickets, ride availability, and trail status are deliberately directed to operators.

## Cincinnati — `cincinnati-market-to-music`

- [Travelling Han, *Cincinnati, Ohio: A 2 Day Itinerary*](https://travellinghan.com/2026/03/11/cincinnati-ohio-a-2-day-itinerary-for-first-time-visitors/) is a first-person account that includes Findlay Market, Over-the-Rhine street art, Washington Park, and Music Hall. It supports the route as a plausible visitor day, not specific recommendations in the article.
- [Findlay Market](https://www.findlaymarket.org/) confirms the market's location, full-time vendors, weekend outdoor activity, and Monday closure. Its [history](https://www.findlaymarket.org/history) establishes the iron-framed house, 1855 opening, and municipal market context.
- [ArtWorks' OTR and downtown mural map](https://artworkscincinnati.org/media/FY21-Printable-Mural-Map_8.5x11-Updated-QR-Codes-2022-copy-compressed.pdf) confirms murals across OTR and walking tours. The map is dated; readers are told to check current tour dates and mural locations.
- [Cincinnati Arts' Music Hall visitor page](https://www.cincinnatiarts.org/plan-your-visit/visit-music-hall) verifies Washington Park directly across Elm Street, performances, and tours.
- [Cincinnati Connector, How to Ride](https://vendorselfservice.cincinnati-oh.gov/streetcar/how-to-ride/) verifies stops at Findlay Market and Washington Park and the connection to downtown. Current routing should be checked before boarding.

## Dayton — `dayton-made-to-fly`

- [Ohio Magazine, *Follow Dayton's Aviation Trail*](https://www.ohiomagazine.com/travel/article/take-off-and-explore-dayton-s-aviation-trail) is an editorial travel route through Wright-Dunbar, Huffman Prairie, and the Air Force museum. It informed a spacious two-day order.
- [National Aviation Heritage Area, Wright-Dunbar day trip](https://visitnaha.com/itinerary/day-trip-wright-dunbar-neighborhood/) gives independent local itinerary context for visiting that neighborhood before moving to the larger sites.
- [NPS Wright Cycle Company Complex](https://www.nps.gov/daav/learn/historyculture/wright-cycle-company-complex.htm) establishes the print shop, Paul Laurence Dunbar's newspaper, the bicycle business, and the neighboring buildings.
- [NPS Dayton Aviation Heritage](https://www.nps.gov/daav/index.htm) establishes the park's joint focus on Dunbar and the Wrights; [NPS fees and passes](https://www.nps.gov/daav/planyourvisit/fees.htm) distinguishes Park Service sites from separately operated Carillon and notes limited Dunbar House tour days. The article gives no fixed admission amount or tour time.
- [NPS Dayton landscapes](https://home.nps.gov/articles/dayton-landscapes-in-aviation-history.htm) and the [National Museum of the U.S. Air Force](https://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Visit/Mus/) support the Carillon Flyer III and large military aviation museum visit. Museum scale also supports reserving a separate day.

## Cuyahoga Valley — `cuyahoga-slow-day`

- [Cleveland Traveler's one-day itinerary](https://clevelandtraveler.com/cuyahoga-valley-national-park-itinerary/) is explicitly written by a frequent visitor and pairs a waterfall, Boston Mill, Peninsula, and trails. The published guide here intentionally selects fewer activities.
- [NPS half-day visit](https://www.nps.gov/cuva/planyourvisit/half-day-visit.htm) verifies the Brandywine boardwalk and viewpoints, Boston Mill, and Ledges as manageable individual stops.
- [NPS parking](https://www.nps.gov/cuva/planyourvisit/parking.htm) notes the Brandywine lot and Towpath access; [current conditions](https://home.nps.gov/cuva/planyourvisit/conditions.htm) carries changeable trail information and explicit safety directions for Brandywine and Ledges. The article asks travelers to check it.
- [NPS trail maps](https://www.nps.gov/cuva/planyourvisit/trail-maps.htm) supports the Towpath as a flexible out-and-back walk rather than inventing a fixed segment distance.

## Sandusky — `sandusky-play-day`

- [Finding the Universe, Sandusky three-day guide](https://www.findingtheuniverse.com/days-in-sandusky-ohio-visiting-shores-islands-cedar-point/) is a first-person travel account that devotes a full day to Cedar Point and separately walks the town. Its July 2026 update was checked; ride counts and recommendations are omitted because they change.
- [Follow the Piper, Sandusky weekend](https://followthepiper.com/how-to-spend-a-perfect-weekend-in-sandusky-ohio/) is a second first-person account supporting the pairing of downtown and a Cedar Point day. It discloses a hosted stay, so it is used for itinerary plausibility rather than endorsements.
- [Cedar Point park map and directions](https://www.cedarpoint.com/plan-a-visit/park-map) confirms the park's Sandusky location and supplies an operator map. Times and availability shown on that page are dynamic and are not copied into the article.
- [Shores & Islands Ohio, Central Shores](https://www.shoresandislands.com/communities/central-shores/) verifies Sandusky's bayfront downtown and Cedar Point setting. Its [Jackson Street Pier itinerary](https://www.shoresandislands.com/blog/post/sanduskys-ties-to-the-underground-railroad/) verifies public waterfront seating and the pier as a gathering place. Its [Maritime Museum note](https://www.shoresandislands.com/blog/post/exploring-lake-eries-rich-maritime-past-at-the-maritime-museum-of-sandusky/) verifies that museum as an optional downtown stop.

## Editorial caveats

- No fixed prices, hours, ride status, or train service promises appear in the published copy.
- The articles are suggested routes, not a claim that the writer completed them. Descriptions of looking, pausing, or choosing are invitations to the reader.
- Attraction conditions and tour calendars can change. The practical fields send readers to the relevant operator before travel.
